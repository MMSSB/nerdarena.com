Frequently Asked Questions

Can anyone post a project idea?

Yes! Nerd Arena is completely open. Whether you are a senior system architect or a junior dev pitching your first vanilla JavaScript utility, you can share your technical ideas here.

Can I upload images or videos to my pitches?

No. Nerd Arena is strictly a text-first and code-block environment. We deliberately omit image posting features to maintain focus on structural logic, technical ideas, and project portfolios without visual clutter.
